Growing a Mo is the most well known way of supporting Movember.  

But you don’t need to grow to save a bro. **Move, Host** and **Mo Your Own Way** are options which may better suit your calling. You can even chop, change and combine as you please.

---

## Grow
Grow a moustache to raise funds for men’s health. It’s the classic and simple approach. Can’t grow one? Even better. The worse the Mo, the more conversations it’ll inspire. It’s how you can literally get in the movement.

[How to Grow](grow)

## Move
Move this Movember by walking or running 60km over the month. That’s 60km for the 60 men we lose to suicide each hour globally. Solo and team efforts accepted.

[How to Move](move)

## Host
Rally a crew and host a trivia night or a ping-pong tournament. Anything close to you or your community’s heart can inspire an event.

[How to Host](host)

## Mo Your Own Way
A choose-your-own-adventure challenge epic in scope and scale. Set your sights on breaking an obscure record. Do dares for donations. Kick a bad habit. Take on a physical challenge. Mo Your Own Way means you make the rules.

[How to Mo Your Own Way](myow)

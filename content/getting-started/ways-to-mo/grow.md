### The place where it all started – growing a moustache on our top lip to raise funds for men’s health
{{< /extract >}}

That means growing a Mo for the month formerly known as November. And it starts by making sure you’re clean shaven using a Gillette razor on the first day of Movember.

----

## Five ways to get more donations when you grow for Movember

{{< counter >}} 
### Shave-down
There’s no better way to kick start your fundraising than to share the evidence of you shaving down on the 1st Movember. Not only will you be ready to start Movember fresh faced and itching to get going, you can also use this as a chance to get the donations rolling in. Live stream, video or get your mates together and shave down in person.

{{< counter >}} 
### Show your mo-gress
Use the Movember app to take a daily photo and share your Mo-gress using our built in GIF creator. Create and share videos and use our filters to splash your ‘tache all over your social media.

{{< counter >}} 
### Dye it
Nothing gets more attention than a pink or purple Mo. Want to raise more? Offer to dye it in colours you hate for extra dough.

{{< counter >}} 
### Mo sweepstake
Auction off the style of your Mo for donations – either to the highest bidder or in a raffle.

{{< counter >}} 
### The Keeper
Not everyone’s a Mo fan. So, what happens if you announce that you plan to keep yours? Maybe it’s an opportunity to get donations to shave it off. Just sayin’.

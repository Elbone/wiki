With fundraising, just like in physics, breaking inertia and building momentum is hard. But also small actions can amount to a big bang. Here are our top tips for newbies and Mo-Pros alike to get a Movember fundraiser gathering momentum like a trout off a waterfall.

{{< counter >}} 
## Don’t overthink it
Here’s the thing about fundraising – don’t overcomplicate it. The lamest moustaches historically raise the most money, so don’t stress about looking as good as Tom Selleck. Just sign up for whatever makes sense to you, just get started.

Whether you [Grow](../ways-to-mo/grow), [Move](../ways-to-mo/move), [Host](../ways-to-mo/host) or [Mo your own way](../ways-to-mo/myow) there is a way to a fundraise for everyone. Choose what is going to be enjoyable for you. Fun is what keeps the Mo on the go.

{{< counter >}} 
## Set goals, and make a plan
Fundraising is easier when you know what you’re aiming for. Pick a realistic fundraising goal or keep it low for now and update it when you hit the goal. 

Then make a simple plan for how you’ll get there. Share your goal publicly so people can help you smash it. And don’t be shy – stretch goals can keep momentum rolling once you’ve hit the first milestone.

{{< counter >}} 
## Donate to yourself
It shows you’re committed and breaks the dreaded ‘$0 raised’ (or 0 Euro, 0 Pesos, or 0 Galactic Credits) barrier, making it easier for others to follow your lead.

{{< counter >}} 
## Ask a friend or family member to donate to you
Friends and family are your biggest fans and are often the most likely to contribute. A donation from them helps build momentum and encourages others to give. Ask them to spread the word that you are doing Movember this year and hopefully gather more donations.

{{< counter >}} 
## Promote your fundraiser
Don’t keep your fundraiser a secret. Share updates on social media, drop your Mo Space link into WhatsApp or Slack groups, and don’t be afraid to ask more than once. People are busy, and often just need a reminder. The more you share your story, the more support you’ll attract.

{{< counter >}} 
## Rally supporters
The magic happens when people connect and combine efforts. Ask friends, collegues, team mates, or other students "What are you doing for Movember?" You’ll be surprised how many people want to collaborate.

{{< counter >}} 
## Bring your workplace on board
See if your workplace will support you – whether that’s a dollar-for-dollar match, a bake sale in the lunchroom, or the boss shaving their head if you hit your target. Office banter + moustaches = fundraising gold.

{{< counter >}} 
## Keep it fun
Wear a silly costume, add quirky challenges, or promise to dye your moustache a ridiculous colour when you hit your target. Fundraising is contagious when it looks like fun.

## Join a team
To join a team, head to the team section of your Mo Space online, or to the ‘Team’ tab of the Movember App. Search for an existing team and ask to join.

## Starting a Team
To start a team, head to the team section of your Mo Space,  or to the ‘Team’ tab of the Movember App. Starting a team makes you the default Team Captain — every team needs one. A legendary Movember supporter. Chief motivator. Champion recruiter. One who leads by example.

**Choose a team name** — Your first responsibility is a team name. Beardless Dragons? The Hairy Marys? Choose wisely. A good name makes recruiting team members easier. The bigger your crew, the bigger your impact.

**Set a fundraising target** — Set a fundraising target with your team and make a plan to blow past expectations.

**Define the team’s motivation** — Like a personal Mo Space, make it meaningful.

**Personalize your Mo Space** — Give your Mo Space a personal touch with pictures, videos, plenty of updates, and team members’ personal stories.

**Keep the feed active** — Activate the team feed with some accolades. Shout out to the person with the best 5 o’clock shadow or who’s moved the most on day one.

**Build your team** — The mo the merrier. Share your team’s Mo Space QR code and encourage your friends, colleagues, and community to join in the fun.

## Fundraising as a team
The best way to raise funds is to build a strong team, inspire them to get involved, and have fun while you’re at it. Get creative – the sky’s the limit.

Team fundraising ideas:
-  Host a Movember kick-off party on Movember 1st with an office Shave Down, morning coffee, themed lunch or go for a team run to kick off your Move.
- Set a fundraising and/or Move target with your team, and make a plan to crush it.
- Ask for donations through email and social media, with a link to your team’s Mo Space.
- Personalise your Mo Space with pictures, videos, updates and team members’ personal stories.
- Add the team’s Mo Space URL to your email signature.
- Use your Mo Space leaderboards to compete with and motivate each other.
- Get your biz to back the Mo by sharing Movember’s health messages and stories through internal communication channels. And don’t forget to ask your employer if they’ll match your team’s fundraising efforts.
- Sacrifice your existing facial hair (beards, goatees etc.) for donations.
- Leave a donation box in your break room, locker room or wherever you gather regularly.
- Give weekly prizes for the best Mo, Mover and fundraiser.
- Auction off the Mo you’ll Grow (meaning others get to make the call on the style of Mo you Grow).
- Get senior members of your organisation on board (we can help with this).
- Throw an end-of-month awards ceremony to toast the top fundraiser, Mover and, of course, the best and worst Mo’s. Make sure the donation box moves around the room.
- Encourage your team mates to download the app to enhance their Movember experience. Start some banter in the group chat, share your Mogress and get wild with our range of Mo-filters.

## Captains
Team Captains are legendary Movember supporters. Change agents. Chief motivators. Champion recruiters. You lead by example, inspiring and motivating others to shake things up and get behind the cause.

You’ll recruit and build a team and together with your teammates, you’ll raise funds and awareness to help change the face of men’s health.

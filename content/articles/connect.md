Starting out with Movember can feel a bit lonely — especially if you’re new, don’t have a big social circle, or aren’t sure how to find other Mo’s. The good news? You’re not alone. Movember is a global movement, and there are plenty of ways to connect with others, build momentum, and feel part of the community.  

Here are some easy ways to link up and find your people.  

{{< counter >}}
## Start small and share your story  
Even if your network feels tiny, start with what you’ve got. Post on social media, drop your Mo Space link in a group chat, or tell a mate over coffee. People often surprise you — someone you don’t expect might be your first donor or even join your fundraiser.  

Sharing your story might just be the best way to drive interactions online — explain your personal connection to the Movember cause. Also consider recording a video of you speaking to maximise the engagement. 

{{< counter >}}
## Join a team  
Teams are the fastest way to go from solo to connected. Search for a workplace team, sports club, or community group on the Movember site. Or start your own team and invite a few friends — even a team of two feels better than one.  

{{< counter >}}
## Jump into online Movember spaces  
Not everyone has local Mo’s to link up with, but the internet’s always open, as is the [Movember app](../../getting-started/movember-app). Join online communities and Movember social channels where you can swap tips, share progress, and cheer on other fundraisers.

{{< counter >}}
## Find an event near you  
From trivia nights to fun runs, Movember events are designed for connection. Don’t worry if you don’t know anyone when you arrive — most people there are looking to connect too.

{{< counter >}}
## Link up at work or school  
If you’re new, you might be surprised who else is quietly Mo-ing. Put a shoutout in Slack, Teams, or a group email: *“Anyone doing Movember this year?”* Chances are, you’ll find instant teammates and allies.  

{{< counter >}}
## Support others, even if you don’t know them  
Leave comments on Mo Spaces, donate a few bucks to another fundraiser, or share someone else’s story. It’s a quick way to feel connected, and often they’ll return the favour.  

{{< counter >}}
## Lean into Movember’s bigger story  
If you feel awkward asking, remember: you’re not fundraising *for yourself*, you’re part of a global mission to change the face of men’s health. Sharing the bigger picture makes it easier to talk to strangers, neighbours, or co-workers.  

---

## Final word: you’re never Mo-ing alone  
Every moustache, every move, every conversation adds to a global movement. Whether you connect online, in your workplace, at an event, or just with one close mate — you’re part of the Mo community. Take the first step, reach out, and you’ll find you’re in good company.

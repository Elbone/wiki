The data is in and there’s no doubt — people who Movember together have more fun and make more impact. The benefits include support, motivation, and collective planning among many others. 

There are many ways to connect with others, here are some:

{{< counter >}}
## Join a team
Teams are a great way to gather around a common fundraising or Move goal, promote healthy (and fun) competiton, and support each other.

People form teams in all kinds of ways — we have teams of running clubs, workplace colleagues, sports teams, and pub mates.

Start by asking around at work or amnong friends and see if anyone has any teams you could join — they’ll very likely welcome new members.

You can create a team in the [Movember App](../../getting-started/movember-app) or on [movember.com](https://movember.com/), or search for a team and join that.

{{< counter >}}
## Join local fundraisers
There are probably people near you already taking part. Sporting clubs, gyms, community groups, and even networks of mates rally around Movember.  

Watch official Movember social media accounts for announcements, or try searching Reddit, Meetup, Facebook groups, or Google. 

{{< counter >}}
## Join an event
People near you are organising events you can attend. Searching your local area for events is a great way to meet other fundraisers and get tips and support.

Among others you might find Shave Downs, gigs, park runs, and quiz nights. 

{{< counter >}}
## Gather your friends
You already know people you like to do stuff with — invite your friends to fundraise along with you and support each other’s efforts, and [form a team](#join-a-team) to promote friendly competition and support each other.

Share with them why you do Movember. If it matters to you, chances are it matters to them.

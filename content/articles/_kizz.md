Tips from Kieran Ryan, Director of Partnerships and New Business at MoHQ Australia.

## Start smart, not hard
Here’s the thing about fundraising – dont overcomplicate it. The lamest moustaches historically raise the most money, so don’t stress about looking as good as Tom Selleck. Just sign up for whatever makes sense to you, whether that’s growing, making a Move, Hosting an event or Mo’n your own way just get started.

Join the MoHQ Australia team challenge. (or the one in your own local market) The Aussie office raised over $110k in 2024, so let’s keep that momentum going!

## Get everyone involved
The magic happens when people connect and combine efforts. Use Slack, chat in the office, ask around – "What are you doing for Movember?" You’ll be surprised how many people want to collaborate.

Don’t forget to donate to yourself first – it kicks off the momentum and shows you’re committed.

## Event ideas that actually work
- Flanno 5K – Kizz has been running this for 9 years where people literally run 5 kilometers in a flannelette shift for donations
- Mo Trash Mo Treasure garage sale – Think auction-style with Kizz as auctioneer. People have sold everything from red wine to odd socks for $50

These are a couple the Aussie’s have run previously for inspiration, but there are plenty more in every market – ask your local fundraising team. They’ll be sharing their tips next.

## Simple stuff that works just as well
- Write your “why” and share the link to your Mo Space – Don’t be scared to ask people to donate.
- Post on Facebook and LinkedIn – Your network wants to support you.
- BBQ fundraisers – Charge people to attend. One person emptied their lounge room, bought some sausages and hamburgers, and people who were hungry paid for their food! It raised serious money.

## Get creative with your moustache
- Mo real estate – Auction off parts of your mo to the highest bidder.
- Decorate the Mo – Let donors decide if you get glitter or a shave-down.
- Themed challenges – Share your move activities and the highest donation nominates what you wear - be it running in heels, flip flops, or going full ballerina.

## Mo Your Own Way
Can be literally anything. Imagine running a marathon a day, swimming 50 laps of a pool, reading for 24 hours straight – whatever floats your boat.

## The numbers game
Start with a $100 target and build from there. The average fundraiser globally hits around $600, but don’t let that intimidate you. Start slow, have fun, and increase your target as donations roll in.

## Have fun with it
Fundraising shouldn’t be stressful. Pick something that resonates with you, set an achievable goal, and remember – it’s about having fun while making a difference. The key is just to start. Everything else figures itself out along the way.

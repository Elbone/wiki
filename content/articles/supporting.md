There’s more than one way to Mo! Here is your list of ways you can contribute to Movember beyond fundraising.

{{< counter >}} 
## Raise funds your way
[Grow](../../getting-started/ways-to-mo/grow.md), Move and Host are very popular and the sky’s the limit when you Mo Your Own Way. Host a quiz night, bake sale, or Mo-lympics challenge. Get creative and use your talents to bring people together while raising dollars for men’s health. Every little thing you do counts.

{{< counter >}} 
## Donate
If you’re not up for running a fundraiser yourself, you can still make a huge difference by donating to a mate’s Mo Space or directly to Movember. Every contribution fuels lifesaving programs in mental health, suicide prevention, prostate cancer, and testicular cancer.

{{< counter >}} 
## Wear Movember merch
Rep the cause with pride. Grab a Movember tee, cap, or hoodie from the [Movember store](https://movember.store) and become a walking, talking awareness machine. Bonus: merch sales also support men’s health projects.

## Raise awareness
Share the Movember message, or share your story. There are almost unlimited ways to do this. Some of our favourites:
- Share one of our [pre-made messages](../../fundraising/social-media-assets) to social media, or roll your own.
- Get on your workplace or school Slack, Teams, or group email and share what Movember is all about.
- Repost Movember content from social media.
- Share a personal story about why you support men’s health – authenticity inspires action.
- Add a Movember banner to your email signature, website, or storefront to spark curiosity and conversations.

{{< counter >}} 
## Start a conversation
Chances are you are here because you care about men’s health, so why not chat to others on this important subject? Ask a mate how they’re really going. Check in regularly. Small, honest conversations can literally save lives.

Conversations are most effective in-person but also matter when you go online. Start a thread, reply with encouragement, or share your experience.

Some useful links:
- [Movember conversation starters](https://au.movember.com/impact#/)
- [How to have a conversation with a man who is struggling](https://conversations.movember.com/en/)

{{< counter >}} 
## Write supporting messages to other fundraisers
Show some love to your fellow Mo’s. Jump on their Mo Space and leave a comment, cheer them on, or thank them for what they’re doing. Fundraising is easier (and more fun) when people feel supported.

{{< counter >}} 
## Support others
Not everyone can fundraise, but everyone can support. Volunteer at a Movember event, help a mate with their fundraiser, or offer your skills (design, baking, logistics, whatever!) to make their efforts go further.

{{< counter >}} 
## Attend local events
Check out what’s happening near you – trivia nights, barbecues, fun runs, and community gatherings are all part of the Movember magic. Show up, bring friends, donate if you can, and spread the word.

{{< counter >}} 
## Be a corporate matchmaker
Encourage your employer to match staff donations, or get the workplace to host a Movember day (think moustache dress-ups, Movember lunches, or a team Move challenge).

{{< counter >}} 
## Be a Mo Mentor
If you’ve done Movember before, guide a first-timer: help them set goals, promote their Mo Space, and keep spirits high.

{{< counter >}} 
## Support year-round 
Men’s health doesn’t stop on November 30. Keep the conversation alive, volunteer with men’s health groups, or check in with mates regularly.


However you Mo, you’re part of the movement.
